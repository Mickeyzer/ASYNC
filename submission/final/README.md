Ishan Bhat and Gaurav Prakash Srivastava

Upload **Spicers_TIET.pdf** and **Spicers_TIET.pptx** in the two slide-upload fields shown by the portal. Each has seven slides including the cover and is below the reported 20 MB limit. Both contain clickable video and repository links on the cover, plus direct architecture/privacy PDF links on slide 5. The supporting PDFs remain separate files in this repository; no extra slides have been appended. This packages the brief's supporting deliverables for access through the available fields, without claiming an organizer-approved exception.
